SLIDE 01 / 05: THE PROBLEM

Manual Sampling is Failing Our Ecosystems

  • Delayed Action: Water testing currently relies on physical laboratory collection, taking 48h to 7 days. By then, contamination has already spread.
  • Low Spatial Resolution: Static monitoring stations miss downstream pollution or localized agricultural runoffs.
  • High Operational Cost: Manual testing costs municipalities thousands of dollars monthly, restricting testing frequency.
SLIDE 02 / 05: OUR AI SOLUTION

Autonomous Edge-AI Telemetry

  • Mobile Drone Patrols: GPS-guided Autonomous Surface Vehicles (ASVs) patrol water bodies, providing high-density spatial mapping.
  • Edge Anomaly Detection: Low-power microcontrollers utilize Isolation Forest models to flag sensor tampering and contamination instantly.
  • Predictive Deep Learning: Cloud-based LSTM neural networks forecast water safety index declines 12 hours in advance.
